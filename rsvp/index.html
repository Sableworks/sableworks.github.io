RSVP Reader

One word at a time on Verse Pro Color.

Speed-reading for EPUB on PocketBook Verse Pro Color (B300). Download the ZIP, copy rsvp.app onto the reader. Default 150 WPM — color e-ink hard-caps at 200. Other PocketBook models are untested.

Download · GitHub

01 — Download

Get the app

GitHub does not allow a bare .app file, so the binary ships inside a ZIP. Unzip it, then copy rsvp.app to the reader. No Docker, no compile.

02 — Install

On the reader

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it to get rsvp.app.
  2. Connect the PocketBook via USB (PC Link / mass storage).
  3. Copy rsvp.app into applications/.
  4. Eject USB — apps often ignore new files while still connected.
  5. Launch RSVP Reader from the applications menu.

After opening a book, playback does not start automatically — you get a pause panel with progress, WPM, and Start. Swipe or the panel adjusts speed in steps of ±10 (cap 200). Stay near 150 unless you have checked a higher rate on your device.
